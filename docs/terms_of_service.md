## 4. Session Terms

### 4.1 Pricing
All counseling sessions are priced at a **unified rate of ₹150 (Indian Rupees)** per session, regardless of the communication mode:
- **Messaging Session**: 10 minutes
- **Voice Call**: 10 minutes
- **Video Call**: 7 minutes

### 4.2 Payments
Payments are processed through **Razorpay**. All transactions are final. Refunds may be issued at our sole discretion in cases of technical failure where a session could not be established.

### 4.3 Anonymity
Seekers are assigned temporary, randomized monikers during sessions. Your real identity is not disclosed to Counselors.

### 4.4 Session Data
Session conversations are **not permanently stored**. Message data is automatically deleted upon session completion. Counselor case notes are encrypted locally on the Counselor's device and are not accessible to SafeTalk.

### 5.1 Peer Helpers
Peer helpers provide emotional support and active listening. They are not licensed mental health professionals and do not provide clinical diagnoses or medical advice.

### 5.3 Payouts
Counselors earn compensation per completed session. Payout details, rates, and schedules are documented in the Counselor dashboard. SafeTalk reserves the right to modify payout rates with 30 days' written notice.
